Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

1. What This Covers

This policy explains what information Night Market TCG ("we," "us") collects when you use the app, how we use it, and who we share it with. Night Market TCG is operated out of Evansville, Indiana.

2. What We Collect

Account information: your email address, chosen username, and password (stored securely by our backend provider, Supabase — we never see or store your plaintext password).

Marketplace activity: your listings, collection, wishlist, folders, orders, and messages with other users, so the app can function.

Shipping information: the address you provide for orders you place or sell, used only to fulfill that order.

Payment information: handled entirely by Stripe, Inc. We never receive or store your full card number. Stripe shares with us only what’s needed to show you order status and process payouts (such as the last 4 digits of a card, or payout totals).

Device and usage information: basic technical data (such as crash reports, if any are enabled) that helps us keep the app working.

Photos: if you scan a card to identify or list it, that photo is sent to Google’s Gemini API, which we use to recognize the card. Google processes that image under its own API terms, which govern how long Google retains it and whether it’s used for any purpose beyond returning the identification result to us. We keep the photo ourselves only if you choose to use it on a listing or add it to your collection.

3. How We Use It

To operate the marketplace: create your account, show your listings and collection, process orders and payouts, and let you message other users.

To keep the app safe: detect fraud, enforce these policies, and respond to reports.

To improve the app: understand which features are used, and fix problems.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties, and we do not use your data to show you ads from outside companies.

4. Who We Share It With

Supabase, our database and authentication provider, which stores your account and app data.

Stripe, our payment processor, which handles all payment card information and seller payouts directly.

Card-data sources (Scryfall, pokemontcg.io, and tcgdex.dev) are used only to look up public card images and prices — we send them card identifiers, never your personal information.

Other users, but only what’s needed for the marketplace to work: your public username, your listings, and any messages you choose to send. We do not show your real name, email, or shipping address to other users — your shipping address is used by the app to generate a label, but a seller only sees what a shipping label normally requires.

Law enforcement or other parties, only if required by law or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Night Market TCG, our users, or the public.

5. How Long We Keep It

We keep your account and order information for as long as your account is active, and for a reasonable period afterward as needed for recordkeeping (such as tax and payment records) or to resolve disputes.

If you report an issue with an order, any photos or documents you send us are kept only for as long as needed to review and resolve that specific dispute, and are used only for that purpose — not shared, sold, or used for anything else.

We keep basic technical logs (such as error reports) to find and fix bugs and keep the app running. We do not disclose or sell this data to third parties.

6. Your Choices

You can update most of your account information directly in the app. To request a copy of your data or request that your account and associated personal data be deleted, contact us at nightmarket.msrptcg@gmail.com. We’ll honor deletion requests except where we’re required to keep certain records (for example, completed transaction records) for legal or tax purposes.

7. Children’s Privacy

Night Market TCG is not directed at children, and you must be at least 18 (or your state’s age of majority) to create an account. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we make a material change to this policy, we’ll notify you in the app before it takes effect.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to nightmarket.msrptcg@gmail.com.
