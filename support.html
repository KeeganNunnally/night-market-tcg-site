Support

We reply to every message.

Email nightmarket.msrptcg@gmail.com for help with your account, an order, a listing, or anything else in the app.

Include your username and, for an order problem, the order number and tracking information.

Delete your account or data

Email us from the address on your account and ask for your account and personal data to be deleted. Completed transaction records may be kept where the law requires it.

Order problems

Message the seller in the app first. If that does not solve it, email us within 7 days of the expected delivery date. Every refund request is reviewed by hand.
