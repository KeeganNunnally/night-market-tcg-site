Unless a listing is explicitly marked as sold by Night Market TCG itself, every listing on the app is created and fulfilled by an independent seller, not by us. We are not a party to the sale between a buyer and a seller — we provide the platform, payment processing, and messaging tools that make the transaction possible.

When you buy an item, you are entering into a sale directly with the seller. Night Market TCG processes your payment through Stripe and passes the proceeds, minus our service fee, to the seller.

Night Market TCG does not inspect items before they ship and cannot guarantee a seller’s description is accurate, though we provide reporting and messaging tools if something arrives not as described.

If an order doesn’t arrive, arrives significantly different from its listing, or the seller stops responding, message the seller through the app first — most issues are resolved that way. If that doesn’t work, email us at nightmarket.msrptcg@gmail.com within 7 days of the expected delivery date (or 3 days of receiving the item, if that’s when the problem showed up) with your order number, tracking information, and photos if relevant.
